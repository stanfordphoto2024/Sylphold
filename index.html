    Why assessment comes after the gym

    A one-shot exam rewards people who test well. The gym builds the capability; the assessment measures a stable profile; validation checks the profile predicts reality. Each layer only means something because of the one before it:

    Practice→Capability profile→Official assessment→Validation→Credential

    The data layer, in public

    The dataset is the asset; transparency is how it stays trustworthy. Everything TQ has recorded from you, the scoring model’s calibration status, and your export button — one place.

    Scoring model · TQ Capability Model v1

    Rule-based trajectory scoring + optional AI semantic judge. Status: v0.1 · unvalidated — weights are hypotheses until the validation loop rejects or confirms them.
    DimensionnαDiscrim. DGrade

    α (internal consistency) > .70 acceptable · D = pupper − plower (top/bottom 27%) ≥ .40 excellent. Computed from your sessions only — real calibration needs the network.

    Your dataset · export
