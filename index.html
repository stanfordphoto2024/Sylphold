How TQ Works

Three levels, one coherent system

LEVEL 1 — TQ GYM

Free, unlimited practice. The easiest assessment environment. Teaches you HOW to collaborate with AI. Gym scores are not official certification scores.

LEVEL 2 — ONLINE CERTIFIED

Controlled, paid, adaptive assessment. Multi-step tasks, verification requirements, decision tradeoffs. Produces a Certified Online TQ.

LEVEL 3 — OFFLINE VERIFIED

Highest-difficulty, proctored, identity-verified assessment from an independent secure item pool. Produces the highest-trust Verified TQ credential.

FAQ

Honest Answers

Is TQ an IQ test?

No. TQ measures human-AI collaboration intelligence. It does not claim to be a general intelligence measure.

Is TQ scientifically validated?

It is designed to measure a construct and is experimental until psychometric validation (reliability, discrimination, validity) exists. This demo shows the architecture for that research.

Is Gym score the same as certified?

No. Gym is practice. Online is certified. Offline is verified. They are three distinct results — never merged or averaged.

Is using AI cheating?

No. TQ measures how well a human collaborates with AI — using AI is the point. Verified TQ adds identity and assessment-integrity controls.
