Device voice

On iPhone or Mac you can make a Personal Voice (Settings → Accessibility → Personal Voice). It is a real copy of you, made on your device — it shows up here.

Match it to you

Talk for 25 seconds and the app will steer the nearest open voice onto your register. It is a voice match, not a copy of you.

How the voice is shaped

Every line is generated with a speed, a register and the exact words to stress. Nothing is sent to anyone else's server.
